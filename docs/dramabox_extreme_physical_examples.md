# DramaBox Extreme Physical Prompts — English

DramaBox two-scene CUT TO: prompts for extreme physical conditions. Scene 1 begins from calm sensuality and heightened perceptiveness. Scene 2 erupts with extreme physical intensity — tension, breathlessness, pain, temperature shock, taste/oral sensation, or surprise/startle.

## Examples

### Example 1 — BRTH (Altitude Sickness & Oxygen Starvation)

**Metadata:**
- Challenge Title: The Last Thin Air
- Challenge Id: 1
- Category Code: BRTH
- Category Name: Extreme Breathlessness
- Subcategory: Altitude Sickness & Oxygen Starvation

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 17-year-old male with a naturally clear, resonant voice that can shift from breathy wonder to raw, strangled effort, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is calm and awed, his breath full and slow, his awareness spread wide across the vast high-altitude stillness.

(breathing softly, eyes lifted) "It's real. The edge of the world... I can see the curve. This is it." (a long, savoring exhale plumes in the cold)

CUT TO:

The same voice now seized by panic and oxygen starvation, each word a jagged, wheezing gasp against a locked regulator.

(clutching mask, voice cracking) "No... no no no—the tank—gone. Can't—breathe—can't—" (strangled inhale through teeth) "—I'm not—dead yet. Not here. Move, you—" (coughing metallic gag) "—breathe... just breathe."

The performance across both moments should feel like a single body ripped from serene mountain stillness into the raw, dissolving terror of suffocation at the roof of the world.
```

### Example 2 — PAIN (Animal Attacks)

**Metadata:**
- Challenge Title: the skull is a rabbit
- Challenge Id: 101
- Category Code: PAIN
- Category Name: Extreme Physical Pain
- Subcategory: Animal Attacks

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 68-year-old male with a weathered, gravelly voice, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is slowing to a jog, chest heaving, a worn gratitude settling in as the dusk air cools his skin.

(breathing deep, almost smiling) "Easy… easy now. Just the trail and me." (whisper, eyes closing) "Yeah… that's it."

CUT TO:

The same voice now has the back of his skull clamped in a mountain lion's jaws, body lifted, mind white with shock, voice breaking into wet, strangled panic.

(voice cracking, gargled) "G—god! NO—GET—OFF—OFF MY—" (choking, teeth chattering) "nnngh—AHH—" (breath rips out in hitching howls)

The performance across both moments should feel like the same man torn from a moment of peace into an animal terror that dismantles his voice entirely.
```

### Example 3 — SHOCK (Animal and Wildlife Encounters)

**Metadata:**
- Challenge Title: Rattler Underfoot
- Challenge Id: 201
- Category Code: SHOCK
- Category Name: Extreme Surprise & Startle
- Subcategory: Animal and Wildlife Encounters

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 28-year-old woman with a warm, breathy alto voice, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is utterly present, barefoot on cool earth, her senses awake and soft.

(a soft inhale, almost a sigh) "This cool earth feels so alive under my skin." (a tender pause, barely audible) "I can hear the night breathing around me." (whisper) "This is everything I needed… quiet, and dark, and just this."

CUT TO:

The same voice now seizes as her bare heel sinks onto a rattler — a raw, shrieking gasp tears out, then a choked, garbled scream.

(a strangled gasp, voice cracking) "Oh god—oh god no—no no—" (panicked, breathless) "Get it off get it off get it off—" (hitching sob) "Please please please—" (desolate, tiny) "Please."

The performance across both moments should feel like a single soul ripped from tender stillness into animal terror.
```
