# DramaBox Extreme Physical Prompts — German

German-language DramaBox two-scene CUT TO: prompts for extreme physical conditions. Scene 1 begins from calm sensuality. Scene 2 erupts with extreme physical intensity. Spoken dialogue is in German (no umlauts — oe/ae/ue substitutions). Directions and speaker descriptions remain in English.

## Examples

### Example 1 — BRTH (Altitude Sickness & Oxygen Starvation)

**Metadata:**
- Challenge Title: The Last Thin Air
- Challenge Id: 1
- Category Code: BRTH
- Category Name: Extreme Breathlessness
- Subcategory: Altitude Sickness & Oxygen Starvation

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 17-year-old male with a light, clear tenor that still carries a boyish softness, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is the calm, awed presence of a teenage climber on the summit ridge of K2, his breath full and slow as he marvels at the curve of the earth in the vast blue-black sky.

(a slow, savoring whisper into the cold air) "Es ist echt. Der Rand der Welt... ich sehe die Kruemmung. Das ist es."

(a soft, pluming exhale of amazement) "So weit oben. Ganz allein. Nur der Himmel und ich."

CUT TO:

The same voice now rips into the raw, panicked gasp of oxygen starvation as the regulator fails, each word a staccato, rattling wheeze of pure survival.

(a desperate, strangled gasp through clamped jaw) "Nein... nein nein—die Flasche ist leer."

(a long, metallic, coughing inhale) "Kann nicht—atmen— (a ragged, whimpering gag) —ich bin noch nicht—tot."

The performance across both moments should feel like a single soul torn from awe into animal terror within the span of a single breath.
```

### Example 2 — PAIN (Animal Attacks)

**Metadata:**
- Challenge Title: the skull is a rabbit
- Challenge Id: 101
- Category Code: PAIN
- Category Name: Extreme Physical Pain
- Subcategory: Animal Attacks

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 65-year-old man with a gravelly, worn tenor, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is softening into a grateful, animal stillness — breath slowing, muscles loosening, awareness of cool air and sweat on skin.

(breathing deep, slowing to a jog) "Leicht... nur leicht jetzt. Einfach der Pfad und ich." (lips parting, relieved exhale) "Nur das und die Stille."

CUT TO:

The same voice now slammed into a frenzy — skull crushed in a predator's bite, world white and dark, throat tearing against itself.

(wet, strangled scream) "GOTT—NEIN—GEH—RUNTER—RUNTER VON—" (jaw locked, hitching gasps) *guttural, broken howls* (teeth chattering) "MEIN—AHHH—"

The performance across both moments should feel like a man dissolving from quiet presence into pure, reflexive animal terror.
```

### Example 3 — SHOCK (Animal and Wildlife Encounters)

**Metadata:**
- Challenge Title: Rattler Underfoot
- Challenge Id: 201
- Category Code: SHOCK
- Category Name: Extreme Surprise & Startle
- Subcategory: Animal and Wildlife Encounters

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 30-year-old woman with a soft, breathy alto, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is calm and dreamy, her body still and open to the forest air, her whisper intimate and unguarded.

(slow exhale, barely speaking) "Das ist alles, was ich brauchte... still, und dunkel, und genau das."

CUT TO:

The same voice now seizes mid-sentence as her heel lands on the rattler — a raw, shattering scream rips from her throat, her words dissolving into panicked sobs.

(gasping, voice cracking with terror) "Oh Gott—oh Gott nein—nein nein—" (choking sob) "Bitte."

The performance across both moments should feel like a lullaby violently shattered by a car crash.
```
