# DramaBox SIT Situation Prompts — Multilingual

Pre-generated DramaBox two-scene CUT TO: prompts from situation-inspired acting challenges. The actor is physically IN a specific situation (body posture, activity, environment, health, pain) that naturally affects the voice. Distributed across English, French, Spanish, and German.

