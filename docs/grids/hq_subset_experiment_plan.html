Experiment Plan — Rescuing the weak emotion LoRAs with VoiceNet-guided high-quality subsets

Companion plan to the 40-emotion sweep. Goal: for every emotion whose LoRA lift is < 1.0, build a cleaner, emotion-supporting training subset filtered by other VoiceNet dimensions + genuineness, then re-tune / re-train and check whether the lift crosses 1.0. Detective mode: one testable hypothesis per emotion.

Why they under-perform (hypothesis)

29 of 40 emotions lift < 1.0 (see LEARNINGS.md). Two likely causes: (1) the training pool was filtered mainly by the target emotion score, so it contains clips that are labelled e.g. "Elation" but are acoustically flat or low-arousal — the model learns a weak, averaged rendering; (2) many are high-valence / subtle states that need the right supporting prosody to read as that emotion at all. Fix: select clips where the target emotion is high and the VoiceNet acoustic markers that define that emotion are present, and genuineness is high (natural, not flat/acted). A smaller, purer set should give a sharper LoRA — and with less data, a lower rank (8/16) should overfit less.

Method (per emotion)

  1. Re-score the candidate pool. For each emotion's clips (natural EmoLia + Got-Talent + DACVAE buckets), run the 99-dim NinetyNineScorer to get the full VoiceNet profile (57 dims), genuineness, blend and the 40 emotion scores per clip. (Natural clips have mp3; Got-Talent codes are decoded via MOSS then scored.)
  2. Filter by a per-emotion recipe (below): target emotion ≥ threshold AND supporting VoiceNet dims in the target range AND genuineness ≥ 2.5. Keep the top-N by a recipe fit-score.
  3. Two training arms per emotion: (A) continue-tune the current best adapter on the HQ subset (short, low LR); (B) fresh low-rank LoRA (rank 8 and 16) trained from scratch on the HQ subset only. Compare against the current best.
  4. Eval with the same 4 metrics (emotion-z, blend, genuineness, speaker-sim) + lift over base. Success = lift crosses 1.0 while blend/genu/speaker hold. Iterate the recipe for stubborn ones.

Training arms & configs

  • A · continue-tune: resume the current best adapter, LR 5e-5, ~1500 seen on the HQ subset (gentle sharpening).
  • B · fresh low-rank: new LoRA at rank 8 and rank 16, LR 2e-4, ~2500-3500 seen, HQ subset only. Hypothesis: purer+smaller data ⇒ low rank generalises better and imposes the emotion more cleanly.
  • Keep the winning arm per emotion by the combined score (z gated by blend≥0.3, spk≥0.1, genu).

Success criteria & logging

  • Primary: lift over base crosses 1.0 (from <1.0) while blend/genuineness/speaker-sim do not regress materially.
  • Every run appended to LEARNINGS.md: emotion · recipe · subset size · arm · rank · z/lift/blend/genu/spk · verdict. Plus the per-dimension min/max LoRA matrix (from the merge-scaling step) so any diff-vector pair is reconstructable.
  • Detective loop: for emotions still <1.0, tighten the recipe (raise thresholds / add a marker) and re-run; log what changed the outcome.

Compute

Runs after the current demo/merge pipeline frees the 8 GPUs. ~29 emotions × (A + B×2 ranks) ≈ ~85 short runs, 8-GPU queue, disk-guarded, resumable. Best HQ adapters upload to TTS-AGI/moss-emotion-loras-40 (v2 path), HQ subsets to TTS-AGI/moss-emotion-train40-subsets.
