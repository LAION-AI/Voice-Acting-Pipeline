# EmoNet Emotion Taxonomy

EmoNet defines 40 emotion categories with synonyms and variations for voice performance annotation. Each emotion can be expressed at 4 intensity levels, enabling fine-grained emotional labeling of speech and vocal performances.

**Reference:** Schuhmann, C. et al. (2025). *EmoNet-Face: An Expert-Annotated Benchmark for Synthetic Emotion Recognition.* [arXiv:2505.20033](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20033)

## Intensity Levels

| Level | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Slightly present | The emotion is faintly detectable, a subtle hint in the voice |
| 2 | Clearly present | The emotion is unmistakably expressed and easily recognized |
| 3 | Extremely present | The emotion dominates the vocal delivery with strong intensity |
| 4 | Very intensely present | The emotion is at its peak, overwhelming all other vocal qualities |
