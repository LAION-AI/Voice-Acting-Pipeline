## Examples

### Example 1

**Metadata:**
- Genre: Anthology & Twilight Zone
- Archetype: Precise, clinical, cold-observation-making Scientific Observer
- Emotions: Disappointment (clearly present)
- Tempo: brisk, elevated momentum
- Arousal: standard baseline energy

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 45-year-old man with a measured, analytical baritone, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is quietly disappointed, reviewing data that failed to meet a precise theoretical prediction.

(glancing at a readout) "Hmm. Die Kurve weicht ab. Gar nicht elegant." (tapping a screen) "Ein systematischer Fehler, nehme ich an."

CUT TO:

The same voice now electrified by sudden discovery, a breakthrough that overturns the previous conclusion.

(leaning forward, excited) "Nein. Das ist kein Fehler. Das ist ein Muster." (pausing, awed) "Es passt zu einer anderen Gleichung."

The performance across both moments should feel like a single scientist moving from clinical resignation to exhilarating insight, with the same precise vocal fingerprint.
```

### Example 3

**Metadata:**
- Genre: NSFW: Confession & Intimate Monologue
- Archetype: Low, seductive, desire-laden Temptation Whisperer
- Emotions: Interest (slightly present)
- Tempo: glacially slow
- Arousal: standard baseline energy

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 40-year-old woman with a low, husky voice that carries a luminous, honeyed warmth, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is casually intrigued, almost lazily curious, as if noticing something beautiful from across a room.

(a cold whisper, recoiling) "Aber du ruehrst mich nicht mehr an. Nie wieder." (a flat, final pause) "Das war mein letztes Wort."
```
