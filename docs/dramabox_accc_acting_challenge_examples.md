### Example 1

**Metadata:**
- Challenge Title: The Last Voicemail You'll Never Send
- Challenge Id: 12072

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 28-year-old woman with a breathy, warm timbre that cracks under pressure, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is on the verge of screaming with joy, phone clutched tight, words already spilling out.

(voice breaking, pressing phone to cheek) "We did it... no, I did it." (barely audible, tears in the throat) "I just needed you to hear me say goodbye."

The performance across both moments should feel like a single breath that gets stolen mid-laugh, then turned into a farewell.

### Example 2

**Metadata:**
- Challenge Title: Caregiver's Bedside Confession to Comatose Soulmate
- Challenge Id: 1681

**DramaBox Prompt:**

```
A 42 year old woman with a gentle, weary alto, delivering this high-quality studio voice recording with no background noise.

The same voice is softly reminiscing, speaking to a sleeping partner with exhausted tenderness.

(whispering, close to mic) "I stopped at that bakery you love. They still had the sourdough." (tiny laugh, then pause) "And I saw a dog—looked just like the one we always wanted."

CUT TO:

The same voice now cracks with held-back tears, confessing her deepest fear for the first time.

(voice tight, almost breaking) "I'm terrified. I don't know how to be without you." (barely audible, pressing hand to cheek) "I'll be okay. I promise. But that's a lie."

The performance across both moments should feel like a single, unbroken conversation with a person who can't answer—shifting from gentle comfort to raw, whispered desperation.

### Example 3

(a shaky breath, voice thinning) "I just wanted you to see me. Not the version you needed." (a quiet, broken whisper) "Just me. Before I learned to hide."

The performance across both moments should feel like a single heart cracking open in real time, from armored control to exposed innocence.
