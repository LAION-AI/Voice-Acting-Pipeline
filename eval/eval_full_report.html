Learnings from Runs 5–9

Cumulative insights from the DramaBox voice fine-tuning campaign.

What Worked

TechniqueFirst UsedImpact
LoRA-merged DiT + fresh AdaLN-ZeroRun 8 Best overall quality. Merging LoRA into DiT and training only AdaLN on top gave clean speaker conditioning without degrading the base model.
FP32 master weights for LoRARun 9 Solved the bf16 ULP floor problem. CPU-offloaded fp32 copies (~2.5 GB RAM) keep Adam updates alive across all epochs. Zero throughput impact.
LoRA on audio_attn1 + audio_ffRun 7 Efficient fine-tuning: rank 64 → 113M params (3.2% of DiT). Trains meaningfully in epoch 1 before bf16 floor hits.
AdaLN-Zero speaker conditioningRun 5 455M-param conditioning network learns speaker identity effectively. Converges in 4–5 epochs with cosine schedule.
Sidon post-processingRun 5 Consistent +0.15 MOS and +0.35 NISQA improvement across all models. Essential for production quality.
ChatterboxVC → Sidon pipelineRun 7 Voice conversion followed by restoration adds +0.07 SpkSim on top of Sidon alone.
Weight debug logging (hash + Δ%)Run 5 Critical diagnostic. Exposed the bf16 freeze (0.0000% changed) that would otherwise appear as normal training loss convergence.
Separate optimizers for DiT vs AdaLNRun 8 Allows different LRs (4e-5 DiT vs 1e-5 AdaLN) and independent freeze/unfreeze of components.

What Didn't Work

ApproachRunProblem
Standard full fine-tune (fp32 master)Run 6 3.5B params at lr=2e-6 did not converge meaningfully in 1 epoch. Too slow, too expensive.
LoRA in bf16 beyond epoch 1Run 7 bf16 ULP floor: weights ~0.01 have ULP≈8.58e-5, Adam updates ~1.72e-5 round to zero. Training freezes silently.
Stacking LoRA on merged LoRA+AdaLNRun 9 Diminishing returns: −0.025 UTMOS, −0.014 SpkSim vs Run 8. Adding more capacity on an already-adapted model doesn't help.
Longer AdaLN-only training (6 epochs)Runs 5, 8 Peak quality at epoch 4–5; epochs 5–6 show mild overfitting (loss rises, diversity drops).

Bugs Encountered & Fixed

BugRunRoot Cause & Fix
bf16 ULP floorRun 7→9 Root cause: bf16 has 8 mantissa bits; for weights near 0.01, the smallest representable step is ~8.58e-5, but Adam updates are ~1.72e-5. Updates round to zero.
Failed fixes: (1) pre-accelerator.prepare() fp32 cast — DDP replaces params; (2) post-prepare() fp32 cast — DDP flat buffers ignore param.data replacement.
Working fix: FP32MasterWeights class: CPU-offloaded fp32 param copies, copy bf16 grads→fp32, run AdamW in fp32, copy fp32→bf16 back.
DDP flat parameter buffersRun 9 Root cause: param.data = fp32_tensor doesn't work because DDP manages its own contiguous buffer. Direct param.data replacement is silently ignored.
Fix: Keep bf16 params on GPU as DDP sees them; maintain separate fp32 copies on CPU for the actual optimizer step.
Accelerator optimizer preparationRun 8 Root cause: When DiT is frozen, empty param groups cause errors in accelerator.prepare(optimizer).
Fix: Use dummy single-param optimizer for DiT when frozen; only step the AdaLN optimizer.
Checkpoint size explosionRun 8 Root cause: Saving full DiT (6.2 GB) at every checkpoint wastes disk.
Fix: skip_model_save=True when DiT is frozen — only save AdaLN (~1.7 GB/checkpoint). Saved ~25 GB disk over 6 epochs.
LoRA weight saving in fp32Run 9 Root cause: LoRA params are bf16 on GPU but fp32 master copies have the real trained values.
Fix: Save from fp32 master copies, not from GPU bf16 params. Verified by checking saved tensor dtypes.
